# Nurses: the field app

Open **Pelo CRM Field** (the app, or the web address on your phone). Allow location access. It works in dead zones: anything you do offline is saved on the phone and sent when you have signal, with the time it really happened.

1. **Your run** lists your hospitals and patients. Red means late.
2. At the hospital, open the request and press **I've arrived**. The app checks you're inside the hospital's area. If you're on site but outside it (large campus), tick the box and give the reason.
3. For each patient, press **Capture bleed**:
   - Photograph the **requisition** and the **patient sticker**. Retake if the app says the photo is blurry.
   - Enter the folder number, ward and bed.
   - Add the tubes, then **Complete bleed**.
   - If you couldn't bleed, choose why (patient refused, unavailable…).
4. When results are released you're told. Take the report to the ward, tick **Report is in the patient folder** and confirm filing (the location is checked again).

Don'ts:

- Don't use fake-GPS apps. The app refuses to continue while one is active.
- Sign out on shared phones. Signing out wipes what's stored on the phone.
