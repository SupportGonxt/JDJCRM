# Departments

You see only the tickets routed to your department.

1. **My department** lists them, breaches first. Each circle is your department's clock, measured in working hours.
2. Open a ticket and press **Acknowledge**. That tells Client Services you are on it and makes you the owner. Managers can assign it to someone else. The clock keeps running until you respond.
3. Investigate, then press **Submit response**:
   - Findings.
   - Corrective action (**Insert canned response** helps).
   - If the time limit has passed, a **breach reason** is required.
4. Press **Return to Client Services**. CS may accept it or return it with a reason. If it comes back, fix it and resubmit.

Escalation, when your clock passes:

- **80%**: amber, a warning to the owner.
- **100%**: red; your manager and CS supervisors are told.
- **150%**: management is told too.

## Pre-Analytical and the laboratory: the Sample desk
- Scan the BLD barcode or the requisition number (a keyboard-wedge scanner works), then press the button for your stage: **Accept sample in**, **Accept into lab**, **Results released**.
- If a stage is late you are asked for a reason. Stages recorded automatically by SkyLIMS show "recorded by the LIS". If one of those was late, add the reason on the bleed page.
