# Management

- The **Dashboard** opens on the **Live** view:
  - today's numbers;
  - the breach register (every stage that went red today, with its reason);
  - active bleeds and open queries.
- **Performance** has filters for date range, region, site, hospital, department, nurse, category and priority. It shows:
  - turnaround and compliance per stage;
  - department response times;
  - volumes, repeat failures and root causes.

  Click any bar or row to see its tickets.
- **Quality** lists corrective-action effectiveness checks: due, overdue, and the effectiveness rate over 12 months.
- **Export to Excel** includes the whole period with every query and bleed. Exports are audited.
- **Wall mode** is a full-screen view for the office TV.
- Daily and monthly summaries arrive by e-mail if you are on the distribution list (ask the administrator).
